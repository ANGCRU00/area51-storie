STYLE – PROGETTO 1 · NOTTE STELLATA
======================================================================
Style: UFO / Area 51 theme. Dark night-sky background (#070514 → #1A0E3D) with small white stars. Lime #B9E62E as main accent (buttons, highlights, glowing borders with soft lime glow). Violet #5B21B6 for secondary accents and hard offset shadows. Light text #F1ECFB, muted text #B3A9CC. Headings in Archivo Black Expanded (uppercase feel, very wide), body in Archivo Bold. Playful comic-book feel: slightly rotated labels, hard shadows, a cartoon flying saucer with a green alien whose lime beam highlights featured products. Mobile-first.

STYLE – PROGETTO 2 · CHIARO CON ACCENTI AREA 51

Style: UFO / Area 51 theme on a light background. Page background #F7F5FC, text #140C2B, muted text #3F3858. Bold comic-book look: white cards with 3px black (#140C2B) borders and hard offset shadows (6px, black or violet #5B21B6), no blur. Lime #B9E62E for main buttons, badges and highlights (always with black text and black border). Violet #5B21B6 for labels, links and secondary accents. Slightly rotated labels, playful but very readable. Mobile-first.
